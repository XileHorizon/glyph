Notes for Android and Mac

Ghost.md

Android

 

Install →

 

  1. Tap Install. Chrome downloads the app.
  2. Open the download. If Android asks, allow installs from Chrome and go back.
  3. Tap Update or Install. Your notes stay where they are.
  4. From here Ghost.md updates itself: new versions arrive while it is open and apply the next time it starts. When a change needs a new app, Ghost.md offers it in the app.

Mac

 

Download →

 

  1. Open the download and drag Ghost.md into Applications.
  2. Open Ghost.md. The first time, macOS says it can’t check it for malicious software: the app is signed by its developer but not yet notarised by Apple. Choose Done, then in System Settings › Privacy & Security scroll down and choose Open Anyway. On macOS 14 or older, Control-click Ghost.md and choose Open instead. You only do this once.
  3. The first time you record, allow the microphone. What you say is turned into words on the Mac itself.
  4. Sign in with the same account as your phone and your notes follow you. From here Ghost.md updates itself, as it does on the phone.

Neither of these? Open the web version.
